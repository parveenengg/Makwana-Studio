THE PRIMEVAL ECOSYSTEM

Wildlife & Beasts

The fauna of Archaia do not exist to be your cannon fodder. Each species operates with specialized perception mechanics: vision arcs, scent detection corridors, hearing thresholds, and territorial instincts.

THREAT: LETHAL

Cinder

CLASSIFICATION: APEX CRATER CARNIVORE · HABITAT: VOLCANIC BASIN

Ancient bipedal apex predator. Possesses extraordinary thermal night vision and an aggressive territorial sweep radius. Avoid frontal confrontations; distract with hurled stone chunks or masked river crossings.

THREAT: TERRITORIAL

Steppe Mammoth

CLASSIFICATION: TITAN HERBIVORE · HABITAT: GOLDEN PLAINS

Roaming herds that migrate south during autumn. Generally neutral unless calves are approached or hunting spears strike. Thick fur and curved tusks can crush wooden shelters in a single charge.

THREAT: HIGH

Forest Raptor

CLASSIFICATION: PACK STALKER · HABITAT: FOREST CANOPY

Agile hunters moving in pairs or trios. One creates sound distractions to draw human attention while the other flanks from behind tall ferns. Fire torches break their offensive coordination.

ANIMAL AI ARCHITECTURE

How Creatures Sense You

Understanding animal sensory logic is the difference between life and death in Archaia.

👁️

VISION CONES

Predators scan forward in wide 120° visual arcs. Breaking line of sight behind boulders and crawling in dense tall grass drops detection to zero.

👃

WIND & SCENT

Wind direction carries your human scent hundreds of meters downwind. Always approach from downwind, or apply crushed swamp mud to mask your presence.

👂

ACOUSTIC FOOTSTEPS

Sprinting creates loud vibrations in the soil. Crouching dampens footsteps; snapping dry twigs or splashing in water immediately attracts nearby attention.

🔥

FLAME REACTION

Most wild beasts fear open flames. Carrying a lit pine pitch torch creates a defensive perimeter during pitch-black nights, but reveals your position from afar.
