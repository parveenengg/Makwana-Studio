THE STUDIO BEHIND THE WORLD

About Makwana

Makwana was founded to build games that honor human grit, composition, and atmosphere. We are an independent studio creating worlds that feel like places, not corporate products.

OUR STORY

Why Stones to Stars?

Before civilization, there was survival.

Modern video games often rush past the most miraculous chapter in human history: the era when an unarmored, frail creature stepped out into an unforgiving primeval wilderness and refused to die.

Without natural claws, venom, or shell armor, our ancestors conquered terrifying odds through observation, patience, and tool craft. Stones to Stars is an ode to that journey — starting in the primeval mud with knapped flint, passing through stone walls and bronze furnaces, and ultimately leading to the edge of the galaxy.

We don't build generic fantasy sandboxes with hundreds of repetitive map icons. We build focused, tense, and atmospheric environments where every resource is precious and every step requires awareness.

COMPOSITION OVER FIDELITY
