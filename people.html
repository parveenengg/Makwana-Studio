THE HUMAN STORY

People of This World

The Stones to Stars franchise is bigger than a single hero. Each chapter follows a generation facing the limits of its knowledge and technology.

SEASON 01 PROTAGONIST

HU

STANCE: IDLE (RESTING & OBSERVING)

Hu is an early human hunter stranded in the First Valley. Without claws, venom, or thick hide, Hu's weapon is awareness: reading tracks in the mud, scent-masking under running water, and crafting sharp flint leverage.
