CARTOGRAPHY OF ARCHAIA

The Living World

A vast, unbroken prehistoric continent. Built on our core philosophy: restrained, painterly art direction with world-class composition. The environment is not a static backdrop — it breathes, freezes, and transforms as seasons cycle.

ARCHAIA · CONTINENTAL SECTORS

Sector: Archaia Primeval Continental Basin

Select any biome below to align environmental lighting and review climate parameters.

01. Forest Canopy

Primeval pine trees, abundant timber, bloodberries, and riverbeds. Prime hunting terrain.

02. Golden Plains

Endless steppe grass, mammoth herds, low natural cover. Dangerous during midday heat.

03. Sandstone Desert

Arid mesa canyons, obsidian deposits, extreme dehydration hazard, nocturnal vipers.

04. Frozen Peaks

Sub-zero blizzards, mammoth bone graveyard, glacial caves. Requires fur coats and campfire.
