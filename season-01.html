THE INAUGURAL CHAPTER

Season 01: Stone Age

“Before cities. Before kingdoms. Before history had a name.”

Season 01 is the opening foundation of the Stones to Stars franchise. You step into the prehistoric valley of Archaia during humanity's most precarious era: when flint and fire were our only shield against apex titans.

SEASON 01 · STONE AGE FIRST VALLEY REVEAL
THE REGION

The First Valley

A self-contained prehistoric valley engineered as a vertical slice of our survival world.

🌲

FOREST CANOPY

Towering prehistoric pines create shadow lines. Thick brush masks your human scent, but conceals stalking predators.

🌊

RIVER GORGE

Carved by glacial runoff. Essential for hydration and cold water baths to throw off scent hounds, but river rapids can sweep you away.

⛰️

HIGH RIDGE

Vantage point with panoramic view of the entire valley. Exposed to bitter winds and lightning strikes during summer storms.

🌋

CRATER NEST

Volcanic basin guarded by Cinder. Thermal vents illuminate the crater, where valuable nutrient-dense eggs are brooded.

APEX PREDATOR THREAT · CINDER

The Cinder Encounter

Cinder is not a boss with a health bar you slash mindlessly. It is an apex carnivore that senses footsteps, listens for snapping twigs, and tracks wind-carried human scent.

Survival requires crouch-walking through underbrush, reading wind direction, holding your breath when its gaze sweeps past, and timing your escape when its attention breaks.
