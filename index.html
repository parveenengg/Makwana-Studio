A PREHISTORIC CONTINENT

The World Is Alive

Stones to Stars takes place across Archaia — an untamed ecosystem where weather, wildlife, and natural hazards operate without caring if you survive. Built on a simple philosophy: restrained, painterly art direction with world-class composition.

🌍

WORLD

Living terrain that shifts with the seasons, from glacial river melt to sub-zero blizzards.

🔥

SURVIVE

Manage body heat, forage calories, track hydration, and find shelter before darkness falls.

🧭

EXPLORE

Chart virgin pine forests, mammoth migratory plains, sandstone gorges, and ice summits.

🪨

ADAPT

Knap wild flint, lash bone spearheads, and shape wood. Cunning is your only leverage.

GAMEPLAY PHILOSOPHY

Survival Isn't a Game

You have no magical inventory and no superhero abilities. Every calorie spent running must be hunted or gathered back. A single broken ankle or hypothermic night is lethal.

THE CURRENT CHAPTER

Season 01: Stone Age

“Before cities. Before kingdoms. Before history had a name.”

You step into the shoes of Hu, an early hunter navigating an unforgiving ecosystem. Your objective isn’t conquest — it’s making it to tomorrow.

01

SURVIVE

Explore a world that doesn't belong to you. Read animal tracks, hide your scent, and sprint when silence breaks.

02

ADAPT
