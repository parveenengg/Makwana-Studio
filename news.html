SEPTEMBER 2026 · DEVELOPMENT

Building the First Valley

We started with a blank terrain and began sculpting the first playable prehistoric valley — balancing mountain silhouettes, river crossings, and dense brush lines for third-person stealth.

FIELD NOTES 013
AUGUST 2026 · DESIGN

Designing Cinder: Animal Perception

A predator shouldn't just be an obstacle; it should feel alive. Tuning hearing radius, sight cones, and stealth crouch thresholds so every encounter feels tense and earned.

Cartography of Archaia

Designing the overarching continent layout. How modular terrain assets and stylized painterly composition allow our independent team to focus on composition, silhouette clarity, and atmospheric mood.

JUNE 2026 · DESIGN

Knapping Flint: Physics of Primitive Tools

Why we eliminated metal weapons entirely from Season 01. How weapon degradation, knapping rhythm, and bound rawhide cords create genuine historical grounding.

MAY 2026 · DEVELOPMENT

Atmospheric Shaders & Day-Night Cycles

Simulating dynamic sun angles through dense pine canopies. The challenge of balancing visibility for stealth gameplay while maintaining eerie pitch-black nights.

Opening the PC Closed Alpha Ledger

Inviting our first wave of external players to test movement physics, stamina burn curves, and predator evasion in the First Valley.
