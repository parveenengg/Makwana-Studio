WHAT IS STONES TO STARS?

The Game

A stylized third-person survival adventure set in an untamed prehistoric world. You are small. The world is enormous. You survive not by superior strength, but by being smarter than what hunts you.

THE CORE LOOP

How Stones to Stars Plays

Every mechanic is designed around tension, observation, and player ingenuity.

STAGE 01

EXPLORE

Read natural silhouettes, water flow, and terrain contours. Chart safe paths across cliffs and dense brush.

↓
STAGE 02

DISCOVER

Find obsidian deposits, natural cave shelters, fresh herb patches, and prehistoric beast nesting grounds.

↓
STAGE 03

SURVIVE

Monitor stamina burn, hypothermia, predator sight cones, and sound cues. Hide your scent in water or mud.

↓
STAGE 04

ADAPT

Knap raw flint into spearheads, cure hide into warmth, lash torches to push back nocturnal carnivores.

↓
STAGE 05

PROGRESS

Establish secure territory, unlock deeper crafting tiers, and prepare for seasonal migration.
